Fixed Scope Configuration and Advisory

Practical Security Improvements That Deliver Peace of Mind

Whether you are a charity looking for hands on help implementing your audit recommendations, or a small business wanting to tighten your defences, we offer fixed scope, straightforward configuration services.

Anti Fraud

1. Email Anti Spoofing and Domain Lockdown

The Risk

Anyone can send an email that looks like it came from your official @yourorganisation.org.uk address. Scammers use this to trick your staff, suppliers, or donors into paying fake invoices.

How We Fix It

We configure industry standard digital signatures (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC) on your domain name so email providers automatically block anyone trying to impersonate your organisation.

✓ The Outcome

Scammers cannot hijack your name, fake invoices are stopped in their tracks, and your genuine emails are less likely to land in spam folders.

Cloud Security

2. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace Hardening

The Risk

Default cloud settings often leave the backdoor open to automated password guessing attacks and give too many staff members full administrative control.

How We Fix It

Using a safe, read only audit, we review your cloud setup to enforce sensible Multi Factor Authentication (MFA), block outdated login methods, and lock down master administrator accounts.

✓ The Outcome

Your everyday office workspace is properly bolted down using the software licences you already own.

UK NCSC Standard

3. Cyber Essentials and Grant Readiness Prep

The Risk

Local councils, NHS trusts, and major grant funders increasingly require proof of cybersecurity before awarding funding or contracts.

How We Fix It

We guide you step by step through implementing the UK National Cyber Security Centre’s (NCSC) five core technical controls, preparing your systems and documentation for assessment.

✓ The Outcome

You qualify for public sector tenders and charity grants with confidence, while often reducing your annual cyber insurance premiums.

Access Control

4. Joiners, Leavers and Access Cleanup

The Risk

Former staff, past volunteers, and forgotten shared accounts often retain access to sensitive files and donor databases months after leaving.

How We Fix It

We audit your active accounts to revoke old access permissions, establish a simple checklist for future staff changes, and roll out secure, easy to use Password Managers (such as 1Password or Bitwarden) for your team.

✓ The Outcome

Only current, authorised people can access your sensitive data, and your staff never have to share passwords over email or sticky notes again.

Data Recovery

5. Ransomware Backup Verification

The Risk

Many organisations think their files and CRM databases are backed up, until an incident occurs and they discover the backups failed months ago or take weeks to restore.

How We Fix It

We audit your backup setup against the industry standard "3 2 1 rule" and run a live, isolated test restore to prove your critical donor or client data can actually be recovered.

✓ The Outcome

Guaranteed peace of mind for your leadership team and Trustees that if the worst happens, your organisation can get back to work quickly.

45 Minute Live Session

6. "Human Firewall" Staff and Volunteer Workshops

The Risk

Even the strongest technical locks can be bypassed if a busy staff member or volunteer is tricked by a convincing phishing email or urgent payment request.

How We Fix It

We run engaging, 45 minute interactive workshops tailored for non technical teams. No boring slide decks, just real world examples of how to spot modern email scams and invoice fraud.

✓ The Outcome

A confident, vigilant team that knows exactly what to look out for and feels comfortable reporting suspicious messages early.

Overall Security Score

Cyber Posture Index 62 / 100
Target Benchmark Low Risk (>85)
Current Risk Rating HIGH RISK
Funding and Grant Readiness Action Needed for Cyber Essentials

Key Risk Indicators Summary

  • Primary Exposure Area: Multi Factor Authentication (MFA) is not enforced across all staff and volunteer Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace accounts, and former leavers still have active logins.
  • Secondary Exposure Area: Daily backups of the donor CRM database have not been test restored to prove they can be recovered after an incident.
  • Funding and Insurance Standing: Currently short of baseline UK NCSC Cyber Essentials controls required for local authority contracts and lower cyber insurance premiums.

Strategic Security Scorecard

Status Focus Area Current Findings and Business Impact Recommended Action (No New Software Required)
[RED] Staff, Volunteer and Admin Access Two step verification (MFA) is optional for 35% of users, and 4 former volunteers still have active accounts. Global Admin rights are shared across multiple daily email accounts, risking donor data leaks if a password is stolen. Switch on mandatory MFA for all accounts, revoke dormant leaver accounts, separate master admin logins, and roll out a secure team Password Manager.
[AMBER] Email Anti Spoofing and Invoice Fraud Domain protection (DMARC) is set to p=none (monitoring only). Scammers can currently impersonate your @organisation.org.uk email address to send convincing fake invoices to your finance team or donors. Lock down your domain DNS records (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC p=reject) so email providers automatically block anyone trying to spoof your charity’s name.
[AMBER] Cyber Essentials and Device Hygiene Antivirus protection is active, but security updates on 22% of staff laptops are more than 60 days overdue, which is an automatic fail for NCSC Cyber Essentials certification. Enable automatic Windows/Mac security updates across all work devices to meet the 14 day NCSC Cyber Essentials standard and qualify for public grants.
[RED] Ransomware Backup Verification Cloud backups run daily, but copies are not locked against deletion, and a live test restore of the donor CRM has not been completed in over 12 months. Configure tamper proof ("3 2 1") backup protection and run a live, isolated test restore to prove to your leadership team that donor records can be recovered quickly.
[GREEN] "Human Firewall" Staff Awareness 91% of staff completed annual training, and the team shows strong awareness of basic scam emails (<5% click rate). Maintain current awareness habits and run a short 45 minute interactive workshop for Finance staff and Trustees focused specifically on invoice and payment fraud.

Next Steps: Phase 1 Action Plan

We recommend prioritising the following practical improvements over the next 14 to 30 days to address the [RED] and [AMBER] findings above without disrupting daily operations:

1. Lock Down Cloud Accounts and Clean Up Leavers
  • Enforce mandatory Multi Factor Authentication (MFA) across 100% of staff and volunteer accounts and remove unused administrative permissions.
  • Close dormant accounts belonging to former staff/volunteers and introduce a simple Joiners and Leavers checklist.
2. Verify and Protect Critical Donor Backups
  • Lock backup copies so they cannot be altered or deleted during an incident.
  • Conduct a live test restoration of your CRM and finance files to give your leadership team documented proof of recovery.
3. Approve Phase 2 Domain and Grant Readiness
  • Sign off on locking down email anti spoofing rules (DMARC p=reject) and automatic laptop updates ahead of your next grant application or insurance renewal.
